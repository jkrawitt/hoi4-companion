# Campaign state (`src/domain/campaign/`)

- `src/domain/campaign/industry.ts` has `industryAt(production, state, techTotals, built)`: factories per type (start states plus instant focus builds plus the levels the construction queue finished by the state's date, `ConstructionSchedule.built`; non-instant focus builds are listed as queued and are scheduled in the queue instead), building slots per state (`floor(categorySlots * (1 + global_building_slots_factor))`, the factor from ideas and technologies) plus the focuses' extra slots, and consumer goods. Focus builds come as `CountryState.construction` (`ConstructionBatch[]`, recorded in reward order by `recordBatches` in `applyReward.ts`: one per header block, with the numbered `state`, the `prefer` states, `random`, buildings, extra `slots` and resources; the country-wide totals stay as they were). `placeBatches` (exported, also returns each batch's `placements`) puts them into the state rows in order: a numbered scope in its state (unplaced if the country does not own it); a `random_owned_*` block in the first preferred state where, after its extra slots, the free shared slots (queued builds reserve slots) hold its shared-slot buildings, else the fitting state with most free slots (lowest id on ties), else the one with most free slots, those two marked `guessed`. Slots are those of the viewed date, while the game decides with the slots of the completion date. Only instant builds count in a row (`fromFocuses` notes what was placed); queued ones are listed in `queued` with their state, and `unplaced` holds what fits no state. **Consumer goods** (`consumerGoodsFor` in `production.ts`, shared with the construction planner) are `consumer_goods_expected_value * (1 + consumer_goods_factor + stability factor)` of civ + mil factories, rounded down, with the define minimums (not checked), capped at the civs. Stability is `state.stability` (the actual value the overview shows; without a start, `stabilityFrom(state.stabilityBeforeModifiers, stability_factor)` kept between 0 and 1). `stabilityFrom` (`campaign/totals.ts`) is the one formula: `stabilityBeforeModifiers` (the scripted part plus `BASE_STABILITY_PARTY_POPULARITY_FACTOR` x the ruling party's popularity, on `CountryState` and every `StateSnapshot`) plus the ideas' and leader's `stability_factor`. Technologies do not change stability (no `stability_factor` in `common/technologies` in the 1.x install); a mod that adds it would belong in `countryStateAt`. Above 50% it gives `stability_good_modifier`'s `consumer_goods_factor` (`common/modifiers/00_static_modifiers.txt`) scaled from 0 at 50% to all of it at 100% (`stabilityConsumerFactor`). All checked in game: Romania 1936 has 69% stability, -7.6%, 32.3% of 18 = 5 factories.
- **`countryStateAt`** (`domain/campaign/countryState.ts`, which only runs the rewards in order and totals them; `applyReward.ts` is what one reward does, as a list of handlers over a `Working` state, so a new kind of effect is a new handler; `rewardScopes.ts` sorts a reward's lines into certain and conditional; `types.ts` has the shapes) applies the rewards of the focuses completed by a date. With `options.start`/`startDate` the start lines are applied first as a step with id `START_ID`, so stat totals and popularity are absolute (start plus plan).
  - **Research events:** `campaign/researchEvents.ts` records, as each reward is applied, the slots (the `add_research_slot` total, which includes the start), the research speed (`research_speed_factor` of the ideas and leader traits, also at each timed idea's expiry), `set_technology` grants (the start's become `startTechs`) and `add_tech_bonus` bonuses (numbered per focus in the order they happen, `BonusRef = { focusId, index }`). `CountryState.research` is that environment, so `countryStateAt(schedule, finish)` gives the whole timeline. `campaign/research.ts` has `planResearch` (reads the environment at the later of the last focus and the last purchase), which settles `has_tech` and the schedule together: pass one leaves `has_tech` unanswered, each later pass answers it (`options.hasTech(id, date)`, from the previous schedule's `researchedBy`) until the schedule stops changing, at most 3 passes. `researchStatusAt` is what the overview's Research section shows.
  - **What counts:** top-level lines and those inside `hidden_effect` or the country's own scope. `if`/`else`/`random_*`/`every_*` blocks are only listed (`conditional`), except that `add_resource`, `add_building_construction` and building slots are always collected: top-level, inside a `random_owned_*state` block (which picks one of the country's own states), and inside a numbered state scope `46 = { ... }` (assumed to be the country's own for now, so add an escape hatch if that turns out wrong). Those are summed per type, with instant builds counted apart. Other countries' scopes, state scopes and other countries' characters are skipped. **Party drift:** `<party>_drift` modifiers (`fascism_drift = 0.1` on a spirit, advisor or leader trait) add that many percentage points to the party a day while active, taken from the others like any change; `campaign/drift.ts` (`accrueDrift`) runs it day by day (one tick per midnight, split where timed ideas expire) between the rewards and purchases that `countryStateAt` applies and up to the viewed date, from the start on (nothing drifts without the start). **Daily drift is scaled above 20% and 30%:** a party above 20% drifts up at half its drift and above 30% at a third (`scaledDrift`, `DRIFT_SCALES` in `applyReward.ts`, used by `accrueDrift`; checked in game on Finland: fascism 19% at the start, 20.80% on 1 March with Susitaival hired 4 February, 26.30% on 11 March, 32.21% on 29 July; the thresholds are read from those cases and what happens past 40% is not known; losses are not scaled). An `add_popularity` effect is not scaled (the coup focus added its full 5 points: 21.3 + 5 = 26.3 on 11 March). The hire day itself does not drift. The overview shows popularity cut to two decimals like the game, with the exact value beside it. **Popularity always adds up to 100%:** once the start has set the parties, an `add_popularity` change to one is taken from (or given back to) the others in proportion to their share, kept between 0 and 100% (the `popularity` handler in `applyReward.ts`; without a start the changes are plain sums). Ideas are added, removed or (`add_timed_idea`) expire, and their modifiers are summed; a leader's traits are the leader's, not one-off stat changes, and their stats are summed into the modifier totals.
  - **Purchases in the state:** `CountryStateOptions.extraSteps` (the purchases) are applied in date order with the completed focuses, a focus first on the same day; `options.politics` (`PoliticsData`) gives slot sizes (an idea added with a `slot` that is full pushes out the oldest in it, so a new law replaces the old one) and seeds the recruited characters, and a `recruit_character` reward adds to them. `CountryState.politics` is `{ held (active ideas by slot), recruited }`, `ActiveIdea` has `slot` / `researchBonus`, and `CountryState.government` is the ruling group. `researchEvents.ts` turns the ideas' `researchBonus` into `categorySpeed`. `planCampaign` runs `planPolitics` then `planResearch` (given the purchases as `extraSteps`) until neither schedule changes, at most 4 passes, with `has_tech` answered from the previous research schedule. `options.ownedDlc` answers `has_dlc` (unlisted counts as not owned).
  - **Adjustments (what the player saw in game):** `domain/adjustments.ts` (types, `adjustmentSteps`, `tradeOverTime`; the store keeps them as `adjustments`, saved, cleared when the country changes, edited with `addAdjustment` / `removeAdjustment`). `adjustmentSteps` turns each into a `Step` for `CountryStateOptions.adjustments` (in `stateOptions`, so `planPolitics`, `planResearch`, `finalState` and the overview all get them through `options`; merged in `countryStateAt` after a focus and a purchase of the same day; `planPolitics` treats their dates as change points and `planResearch` reads the environment no earlier than the last one). A stability entry (`StabilityAdjustment.value` is a fraction) is a `set_actual_stability` line: the `setActualStability` handler in `applyReward.ts` adds `target - current` to `add_stability`, `current` being `rawCountryStat` (unclamped) at that moment, so the actual stability is the target on that date whatever the plan did before, and later rewards move on from it. An event modifier is an `add_timed_idea` (or `add_ideas` without days) named `adjust:<id>` with one modifier line from `ADJUSTABLE_STATS`, so it shows among the national spirits and is summed and expires like a real one. `finalState` is taken at the later of the plan's end and the last adjustment, so the construction factors see them. The UI is `components/overview/AdjustmentsPanel.vue` under the date scrubber, and the timeline lists them as `adjustment` entries.
  - **Conditions:** `resolveConditions` (`campaign/conditions.ts`) settles each `EffectLine.check` against what the campaign knows when the focus completes (only a chain in the country's own scope: top level, `hidden_effect`, the country's tag, `ROOT`/`THIS` and branches of such chains; inside `every_other_country`, another country, a state scope or a character the limit asks about something else, so the chain is left as written). `annotateConditions` makes the same decision per chain (one `readChain` / `decideChain` for both) but keeps every line and marks `EffectLine.branch` (`applies` on the header of the branch that runs, `skipped` on every line of the branches that do not, nothing from an unanswered branch on); `applyReward` computes it with the same context for each planned focus and `CountryState.focusEffects` holds it by focus id (not for the start, purchases or adjustments). It answers: ideas active, focuses completed earlier in the plan, the ruling party so far, popularity and `has_war_support` / `has_stability` (`CountryState.stability` / `warSupport`, see `countryStat`) once the start has set them, `original_tag` / `tag`, `has_dlc` (`ownedDlc`), `always`, and who leads. `checkOfLines` (`effects/conditions.ts`) reads a whole trigger block (an `available` or `allowed`), where `if`/`else_if`/`else` chains inside a trigger are read as `any(not limit, body)` / `any(all(limit, body), all(not limit, else))`; `hidden_trigger` and `custom_trigger_tooltip` are read as their terms (all required); `has_war` is a `war` check that only the political power planner answers (`atWar: false`, purchases are planned for peace), so focus rewards leave it unknown; the politics import writes scripted triggers (`common/scripted_triggers`, e.g. `PHI_SEA = yes`) out in place before reading `available`/`allowed` (`expandTriggers` in `parsers/politics.ts`); a term like `not_already_hired_except_as` is `unknown`. It uses the state before the focus's own effects, so a popularity change and an `if` on it in the same reward see the old value. Evaluation is three-valued (`evaluateCheck`: true, false or undefined; one decisive term settles an `all`/`any` beside an unknown one). The branch that applies replaces the whole chain and counts like any other lines; a chain that cannot be settled stays from its first unanswered branch on (a leading `else_if` becomes an `if`) and is listed as conditional. `has_tech` is answered from `options.hasTech` (the research schedule, see above; without it the branch stays conditional). **State control:** `controls_state` / `has_full_control_of_state` / `owns_state = <id>` (a `state` check) is true for the states in `options.ownedStates` (the importer's start states) and unknown otherwise, since claims, wars and transfers are not tracked (a state held at the start is assumed kept). **Country flags:** `set_country_flag` / `clr_country_flag` are kept on the line with `EffectLine.bookkeeping` (not shown: `EffectList.vue` filters them, `pruneEmptyBlocks` ignores them), and the `countryFlags` handler records them in `Working.flags`, so `has_country_flag = X` (a `flag` check) is true or false once a reward of the plan has set or cleared it, unknown before (events and other countries' scripts can set flags too; the block form with `days` is unknown). `ROOT = { ... }` in a condition is read as its contents (ROOT is always our country). **Wars are entered by hand** (`WarAdjustment` in `adjustments.ts`: from a date, the enemy tags, or none for peace; replaces the entry before; applied as a `set_enemies` line by the `setEnemies` handler into `Working.enemies` / `CountryState.enemies`): `has_war` and `has_war_with = TAG` (`war_with` check) are answered from them, and stay unknown until the first entry, so the plan never assumes a war (no hardcoded dates; the political power planner still plans for peace). Not answered yet: factions and other countries' state (their leaders).
  - **Leaders:** `has_country_leader = { id = N }` / `{ character = X }` is answered from `identity` (`create_country_leader` carries its `id` as `EffectLine.leaderId`; `promote_character` and `add_country_leader_role` give a character; it is unknown again after `retire_country_leader`, since the game picks the next leader). A character never matches a legacy id, so in a 1936 start `id = 550` (Carol II, only created in the `1939.1.1` block) is false. The game picking a new leader is modelled from `ImportedCountry.leaders` (`LeaderData`: the recruited characters with a `country_leader` role, in order, as named `promote_character` lines grouped by ideology group, plus the sub-ideology to group map; passed on as `options.leaders`): when a reward retires the leader, or changes the ruling party to one the leader does not lead for, and does not make a leader itself, the leader becomes the first candidate of the ruling party who has not been retired. `CountryState.leader` is `{ name, ideology, traits }`, and the overview shows it in its own Leader section.
- `src/domain/campaign/timeline.ts` has `buildTimeline`: every planner's schedule merged into game days, each with what finished (`done`) and what starts (`todo`, the player's next actions; focus builds are `automatic`), plus counts of what never happens. Every entry has an `id` with no date in it (`focus:<id>`, `research:<tech>`, and for the queues the player orders, the item's own `id` from `domain/queue.ts` `withIds`, given on add and on load, kept through moves and edits; `moveItem` there is the one move up/down of every queue), so the actions the player checks off as done in game (`checkedOff` in the store, saved with the campaign, cleared when the country changes) stay checked when the plan moves. The store exposes it as `timeline`; `wholeDay` in `calendar.ts` is the day an instant falls on.
