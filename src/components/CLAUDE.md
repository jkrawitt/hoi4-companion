# Routes and components (`src/components/`, `src/views/`)

- Routes (`src/router/index.ts`, hash history so reloads work on GitHub Pages): `/` is `views/OverviewView.vue` (date scrubber, `AdjustmentsPanel.vue` (stability and event modifiers seen in game), government, national spirits, one Modifiers section: `combineModifiers` in `campaign/modifiers.ts` merges the spirits/leader/laws/staff totals, the technology totals and the rules into one row per stat key (never two keys together, zero totals dropped) under the groups of `statGroups.ts` (`STAT_GROUPS`, `statGroupOf`: ordered key patterns, first match wins, `other` last; industry is split into economy and trade (consumer goods, resources to market), construction (speeds, slots, conversion) and production (output, efficiency)), with a note of each part when both sources add to a stat; `components/overview/`) and `/focus` is `views/FocusPlanView.vue` wrapping `components/focus/FocusPlanner.vue` (the plan: a list grouped under completion-year banners, showing each focus's dates and completion effects; focuses are added and removed only through the graph). `/research` is `views/ResearchPlanView.vue` wrapping `components/research/ResearchPlanner.vue` (the queue: a list in queue order under completion-year banners, with each row's dates, slot, days, issues, a bonus dropdown from `availableBonuses`, and move up/down gated by `isValidResearchPlan`) and `ResearchFolderGraph.vue` (folder tabs, cards on the folder grid with SVG connectors; click toggles through `pathToTech` / `removeWithDependents`; the tooltip lists the stats). `/timeline` is `views/TimelineView.vue` wrapping `components/timeline/CampaignTimeline.vue` (days under year banners with Done and Next lists, a done-in-game checkbox on each Next action and a Hide checked toggle that also drops days left with nothing to do, kind filters, date links to the overview). `/production` is `views/ProductionView.vue` (the date scrubber shared with the overview, `components/production/TradePanel.vue` (imports and civs received from trade), tiles for civ/mil/dock factories, consumer goods, available civs and slots, a states table with a From focuses column whose rows have a `+` that fills the add row, a list of unplaced builds, and `components/production/ConstructionQueue.vue`: an add row limited by `levelsLeft` (`state_max`), rows in queue order with dates, civs, days and issues, move up/down and remove; focus-queued rows are greyed and fixed). `/politics` is `views/PoliticsPlanView.vue` wrapping `components/politics/PoliticsPlanner.vue` (left, the four groups of the politics screen with their slots, what is held on the overview's date, and the options with their cost now and a tooltip of availability, research bonus and modifiers; click queues a purchase, a held advisor offers dismiss. Right, the queue under year banners with date, cost, balance after, issues, a not-before date and move/remove). The overview has a Political power section (balance, daily gain, next purchase, and laws/advisors/designers by slot, so national spirits only list ideas without a slot). `App.vue` has the header, country selector and nav, and keeps pages alive (`KeepAlive`) so switching keeps scroll and state; each view needs a single root element for that. Shared between components: `components/overviewLink.ts` (`useShowOnOverview`, what a row's date links to), `components/yearBanners.ts` (the year banner above the first row of each year) and `src/styles.css` (the global `.muted` and `.year-banner`; every other style is scoped). `describeAhead` in `research.ts` is the ahead-of-time text of the folder graph and the queue. A plan row's end date opens the overview on that date (`completionDay`). `components/GameDataImport.vue` is the folder picker.
