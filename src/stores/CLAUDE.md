# Campaign store (`src/stores/`)

- `src/stores/campaign/` (`useCampaign()`, from `index.ts`) is the one shared campaign: country, imported focuses and research data, plan, research queue (`researchPlan`, saved too; kept when still valid, cleared when the country changes), construction queue (`constructionPlan`, saved too, kept when `isValidConstructionPlan`, cleared when the country changes; `constructionSchedule` is its schedule from `planConstruction`, and `industry` includes the levels built by `viewDate`), political power purchase queue (`politicsPlan`, saved too, filtered to known options on load, cleared when the country changes; edited through `addPurchase`, `movePurchase`, `removePurchase`, `setNotBefore`, `resetPolitics`), start date, speed bonus, the overview's `viewDate`, import status, and `production` and the computed `industry` (`industryAt` at the viewed date), `researchSchedule` / `researchEnvironment` / `researchStatus` and `politicsSchedule` (all via `planCampaign`; the overview's `state` uses its `hasTech` and the purchases as `extraSteps`). It is a module-level singleton, so every page shares it. The parts: `index.ts` (the settings refs, the wiring, the country-change and save watches, `completionDay`/`formatDate`/`parseDate`), `saved.ts` (localStorage), `gameData.ts` (`createGameData`: imported data and the import), `queues.ts` (`createQueues`: the queues and their edits, `keepValid`), `planning.ts` (`createPlanning`: everything computed) and `dates.ts` (the date helpers). `researchTotals` is what the technologies the country has on the viewed date add up to (`techModifierTotals` in `campaign/research.ts`: the plain stats of the start techs, focus grants and finished research, summed); the overview shows it as Modifiers from technology. `planEnd` is the end of the whole plan (the latest of the last focus, research and construction level and purchase); the overview's date slider runs to it, and a view sitting at the end follows it. The plan and settings are saved to localStorage (`hoi4-companion:campaign`); focuses stay in the IndexedDB cache. Every view of the country (`stateOn`: the overview's `state`, the construction's `finalState`, the planners) goes through `stateOptions`, which holds the start, leaders, laws, `politics` and the adjustments, so none of them can miss one. A saved plan is kept when the focuses load and still valid, and cleared when the country changes.
